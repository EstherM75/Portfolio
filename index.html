About

Professional Biography

Esther Gonzales is a journalist, editor, researcher, and human rights consultant whose work bridges journalism, Holocaust studies, testimony preservation, educational outreach, and literary editing.

She earned a Bachelor of Arts in Journalism from Ramapo College of New Jersey and later completed a Master’s degree in Holocaust and Genocide Studies at Kean University.

Esther currently serves as an Academic Specialist at Kean University, conducting research focused on community facilitation of child and youth resilience during periods of war, rebellion, and armed conflict. Her work includes survivor-centered curriculum development, Holocaust testimony education initiatives, and educational resource creation for use throughout New Jersey.

Through journalism, public scholarship, conference presentations, editorial work, and educational advocacy, Esther’s work centers around memory, resilience, testimony, and the preservation of stories.
